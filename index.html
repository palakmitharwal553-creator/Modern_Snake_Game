How to play

  1. Collect the numbers in order: 1, then 2, then 3, and keep counting.
  2. Each number you eat adds 1 to your score and grows your snake.
  3. The game speeds up as your score climbs, up to a playable limit.
  4. Normal: touching a wall or your own body ends the game.
  5. No walls: the edges wrap around. You only die on your own body or a bomb, but the game speeds up sooner and each number adds 2 segments.
  6. Ranks: your score decides your rank: Rookie, Explorer (10), Skilled (25), Pro (50), Expert (80) and Legend (120).
  7. Certificate: you earn a certificate at 50 (Pro), 80 (Expert) and 120 (Legend) points. Enter your name on the game over screen and download it.

Power-ups

  • Stopwatch slows the game to level 1 or 2 speed for about 35 seconds. Scoring and growth carry on as usual.
  • Plus lets you pass through walls for 12 seconds (Normal mode only). You come out on the opposite side.
  • Bomb ends the game if you eat it. It disappears on its own after a few seconds.

Controls: arrow keys or WASD to steer, Space or P to pause. On the start screen, left / right (or 1 / 2) switches mode. On a touch screen, swipe on the board or use the arrow pad.
